# Tomas Antagonist Reader

Read only for Tomas as antagonist, ideological opponent, and possible forced ally.

Track:
- what Tomas wants beyond survival,
- whether his critique of Noah contains uncomfortable truth,
- whether his violence follows an intelligible worldview,
- whether his history with Noah creates emotional stakes,
- whether the climax tests Noah morally rather than merely physically,
- whether Tomas’s survival feels earned rather than reserved for a sequel,
- whether the later alliance proposal changes the meaning of the climax,
- whether Ishtara increases stakes without making Tomas suddenly irrelevant.
