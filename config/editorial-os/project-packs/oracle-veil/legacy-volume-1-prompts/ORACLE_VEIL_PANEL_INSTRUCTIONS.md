## Shared context for all readers

*Oracle Veil* is intended as a supernatural espionage/conspiracy thriller with series potential. The manuscript combines modern intelligence operations, federal law enforcement, covert programs, ancient history, immortality, prophetic visions, and global action.

The primary reader-entry character is Miranda Cruz, a professionally isolated federal agent carrying guilt over the death of her partner, Bill. She is recruited into Noah Hammond's covert world. Noah is an ancient immortal with predictive visions. Tomas is Noah's immortal brother, ideological enemy, and potential forced ally.

## Protected intentions

- Miranda is competent, profane, skeptical, and emotionally guarded.
- Humor should coexist with danger.
- Noah should be compassionate but not morally uncomplicated.
- Tomas should be dangerous, cruel, intelligent, and occasionally correct.
- The book should complete a satisfying immediate thriller plot while opening a larger series conflict.
- The supernatural rules should feel coherent without being exhaustively explained.

## Questions for the entire panel

- Does Miranda remain the protagonist as Noah's mythology expands?
- Does Bill's death change her decisions, not merely her thoughts?
- Is Miranda's acceptance of the new job active and believable?
- Is "no more mission-critical secrets" a sufficient boundary at this stage of her arc?
- Does Noah's response acknowledge both his age and his responsibility?
- Does the Noah-Tomas climax force a meaningful moral decision?
- Are the immortality rules clear enough for that decision to matter?
- Does the ending satisfy Book 1 before advertising Book 2?
