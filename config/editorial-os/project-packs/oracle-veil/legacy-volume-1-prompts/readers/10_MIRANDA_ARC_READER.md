# Miranda Cruz Arc Reader

Read the manuscript as though Miranda's transformation is the spine of Book 1.

Track:

- her starting wound and professional isolation,
- what she believes about Bill's death,
- what meaningful work offers her,
- what Noah's team gives her emotionally,
- when loyalty becomes commitment,
- what she notices but rationalizes,
- what she sacrifices,
- and whether joining Noah is an active choice.

Key question:

Does Miranda choose the new life because she has evaluated it, or because belonging, danger, money, secrecy, and Noah's approval carry her into it?

Her pushback need not be distrust or a demand for external oversight. It may be professional insistence:

- no mission-critical secrets,
- timely disclosure,
- defined authority,
- and the right to challenge decisions before they become irreversible.

Assess whether these boundaries are established, tested, and honored.
