# Tomas Antagonist Reader

Evaluate Tomas as antagonist, brother, ideological rival, and possible temporary ally.

Track:

- what he wants,
- what he believes about humanity,
- what he wants from Noah,
- what he knows that Noah does not,
- where he manipulates events,
- where he tells the truth,
- and why he preserves Miranda or the team.

Assess whether his apparent help feels retrospectively supported rather than conveniently revealed.

Determine whether the climax tests his and Noah's physical limits, moral limits, or both. The strongest version should make the fight inseparable from their history and competing philosophies.
