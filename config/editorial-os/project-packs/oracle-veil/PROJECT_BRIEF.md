# Oracle Veil Project Brief

## Format
Commercial supernatural/conspiracy thriller; Book 1 of a planned series.

## Central promise
A guilt-ridden but forensically cleared federal agent is drawn into a covert program led by an ancient immortal and must help stop an attack while discovering that the program, its history, and its moral authority are far less transparent than she believed.

## Primary protagonist
Miranda. Her strongest material combines competence, guilt, suspicion, anger, and a growing need to decide whether purpose justifies surrendering autonomy.

## Core relationship
Miranda and Noah. Their relationship should evolve from recruitment and guarded trust toward operational partnership complicated by secrecy, power imbalance, immortality, and institutional dependence.

## Current targeted concern
When Miranda accepts Noah’s offer, she has not yet developed a full critique of the program or demanded external oversight. Her pushback should therefore arise from professional competence and personal dignity: no mission-critical secrets, timely disclosure, defined authority, and the right to challenge decisions.

## Noah response test
His line about having lived for thousands of years can be wry and revealing, but it must not function as an exemption from accountability. He should acknowledge the standard, accept that omissions have consequences, or demonstrate later that he is trying to change.

## Tone
Fast, serious, occasionally dry or darkly funny. Avoid turning ancient perspective into constant grandiosity.
