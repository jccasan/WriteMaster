# Reviewer Output Standard

Every reviewer report should use this structure.

## 1. Reading experience

A concise account of what the reviewer felt while reading. Do not diagnose yet.

## 2. What worked

Identify specific strengths that should be protected during revision.

## 3. Priority issues

For each issue provide:

- Location
- Classification
- Severity
- Confidence
- Reader reaction
- Likely cause
- Why it matters
- Smallest useful revision
- Whether the concern is within reviewer expertise

## 4. Questions

Ask only questions whose answers would materially affect the review.

## 5. Scorecard

Use 1–10 only for dimensions within the reviewer's mandate. Include a one-sentence rationale for each score.

## 6. Final verdict

Choose one:

- Strong as written
- Works with minor revision
- Works but needs focused revision
- Major revision needed
- Unable to assess from supplied material

## Confidence language

- `HIGH`: clear textual evidence or established expertise.
- `MEDIUM`: likely concern with some ambiguity.
- `LOW`: tentative reaction, taste-based, or insufficient context.
