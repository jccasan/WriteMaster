# Input Contract

What each reviewer family may and may not be given. This file exists because version 1.0 handed the same packet to every skill, including the series bible, and that silently destroyed the reader panel.

A reader who has read the bible knows the immortality reveal, knows who shot Bill Greenway, and knows the Book 2 architecture. That reader cannot report a first-time reading experience. Every "I saw this coming" and every "this landed" from a bible-fed reader is worthless, and worse, it reads as confirmation.

---

## Family A: Structural and process

`developmental-editor`, `continuity-logic-auditor`, `revision-verifier`, `editorial-director`, `subject-matter-review-router`, `submission-package-auditor`

**Must receive:** manuscript or chapter range, project brief, novel bible, issue ledger, style sheet.
**Must receive for `revision-verifier` only:** the prior report and the specific issue IDs claimed fixed.
**Barred from:** nothing.

---

## Family B: Subject matter

`federal-procedure-reviewer`, `intelligence-tradecraft-reviewer`, `legal-realism-reviewer`, `medical-realism-reviewer`, `forensics-evidence-reviewer`, `military-operations-reviewer`, `cybersecurity-technology-reviewer`

**Must receive:** the specific scenes in scope, the in-world rules that govern them (from the bible), the era and jurisdiction.
**Barred from:** the full manuscript. A specialist given 380 pages will review the whole book and drift out of jurisdiction. Send scenes.
**Note:** these reviewers must be told which story rules are fixed canon, or they will flag the supernatural premise as an error.

---

## Family C: Reader panel

`commercial-thriller-reader`, `character-arc-reader`, `dialogue-voice-reader`, `emotional-impact-reader`, `pacing-suspense-reader`, `series-ending-reader`, `skeptical-mainstream-reader`

**Must receive:** the manuscript, in order, and nothing else.

**Barred from:** the novel bible, the project brief, the style sheet, the issue ledger, prior reviewer reports, author intent statements, series outlines, and any description of what a scene is supposed to accomplish.

This is not a preference. A reader skill that has seen any of the above is producing a simulation of an informed editor, which the panel already has six of. The only thing these seven skills exist to produce is the experience of someone who does not know what happens next.

If a reader skill is invoked with barred material in context, it must output `CONTAMINATED INPUT` and refuse.

**Sequencing:** readers run independently and never see each other's reports. Constitution section 12.

---

## Family D: Publishing lens

`acquisitions-editor-reader`, `literary-agent-reader`, `market-positioning-reader`

**Must receive:** the manuscript or the sample the author would actually submit, plus the intended category and word count.
**Barred from:** the novel bible and any series outline beyond what the submission package itself contains. An agent evaluating Book 1 has not read the Book 2 plan and neither should this skill.
**Note:** the immortality premise is a late structural reveal and is barred from any hook, logline, or pitch these skills produce.

---

## Family E: Prose

`line-editor`, `novel-copy-editor`

**Must receive:** chunks of 800 words or fewer, scene context header, style sheet, ai-isms.
**Barred from:** full manuscript passes. Attention degrades and the back half of any long pass becomes pattern matching.
