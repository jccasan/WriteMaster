# Noah Hammond Character Reader

Read for Noah's moral complexity, age, leadership, secrecy, and fear of final death.

Evaluate whether he feels like a person who has lived for millennia rather than a contemporary wealthy operative with historical references.

Track:

- how age affects memory,
- patience,
- attachment,
- grief,
- risk tolerance,
- humor,
- paternalism,
- and ethical compromise.

Pay special attention to his response when Miranda demands transparency. A line such as, "I've been alive for thousands of years. You'll have to forgive me if I forget something," may work as humor and character texture, but it must not erase accountability.

Assess whether he follows the joke with an actual commitment and whether later behavior proves or violates it.
