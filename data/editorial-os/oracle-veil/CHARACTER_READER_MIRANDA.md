# Miranda Character Reader

Read only for Miranda’s credibility and arc.

Track:
- guilt from Bill’s death and how it changes behavior,
- professional competence under extraordinary conditions,
- how quickly she accepts Noah’s worldview,
- whether curiosity, anger, and loyalty coexist,
- what she asks before accepting ORION,
- whether she has meaningful choices,
- dependence on Noah for information, livelihood, safety, and legitimacy,
- the difference between believing in the mission and trusting the institution,
- whether the ending turns unease into a concrete future conflict.

Pay special attention to the job-offer scene. Her boundary should be appropriate to her current awareness: “Tell me what I need to know before it affects my decision, not after.” Do not force her to articulate a mature external-oversight argument before the manuscript has earned it.
