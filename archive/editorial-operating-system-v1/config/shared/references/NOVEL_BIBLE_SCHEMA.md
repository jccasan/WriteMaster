# Novel Bible Schema

The novel bible stores established facts. It is not a dumping ground for every sentence in the manuscript.

## Project identity

- Title
- Series title and book number
- Genre and subgenre
- Intended audience
- Tone promise
- Content boundaries
- Draft version and date

## Story contract

- Central premise
- Primary protagonist
- Protagonist's external objective
- Protagonist's internal need
- Central opposition
- Main stakes
- Reader promises
- Questions intentionally withheld

## Character records

For each recurring character:

- Canonical name and aliases
- Age or apparent age
- Pronouns
- Physical details
- Occupation and expertise
- Backstory facts
- Wants
- Fears
- Secrets
- Moral boundaries
- Voice markers
- Important relationships
- Knowledge by chapter
- Injuries and lasting consequences
- Arc state by act

## Timeline

For every chapter or scene:

- Date
- Time
- Location
- POV
- Elapsed time since previous scene
- Travel duration
- Injuries carried forward
- Information gained
- Decisions made
- Open causal dependencies

## World and rule systems

- Supernatural or speculative rules
- Costs and limitations
- Known exceptions
- Who knows each rule
- When the reader learns it
- False beliefs held by characters

## Organizations

- Name
- Public purpose
- Actual purpose
- Hierarchy
- Authorities
- Resources
- Internal factions
- Relationship to government or law
- Information security practices

## Operational and technical facts

- Weapons
- Vehicles
- Communications
- Surveillance
- Travel
- Medicine
- Legal authority
- Intelligence sources
- Procedures

Only store facts established in the manuscript or deliberately supplied by the author. Label uncertain items.

## Mystery ledger

For every mystery:

- Question
- First appearance
- Clues planted
- Misdirections
- Intended answer
- Reveal location
- Reader knowledge
- Character knowledge
- Status: open, partially answered, answered, deferred

## Style and language

- English variant
- Dialogue punctuation
- Thought formatting
- Number style
- Capitalization
- Foreign-language treatment
- Invented terminology
- Profanity norms
- POV and tense rules
