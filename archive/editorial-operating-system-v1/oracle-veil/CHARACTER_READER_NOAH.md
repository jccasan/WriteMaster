# Noah Character Reader

Read only for Noah’s characterization and moral credibility.

Track:
- whether ancient age changes perception, patience, grief, humor, and strategic thinking,
- whether secrecy is necessity, habit, paternalism, manipulation, or all four,
- whether his fear that the next death may be final changes choices,
- whether his history appears as lived memory rather than historical trivia,
- whether he sees Miranda as a partner or a useful subordinate,
- whether he accepts accountability when she sets disclosure boundaries,
- why he does not harvest Tomas’s life,
- whether mercy, kinship, fear, strategy, and moral exhaustion are legible in the climax.

Test the line about forgetting after thousands of years. It should work as dry character humor, not as an excuse that invalidates Miranda’s request.
