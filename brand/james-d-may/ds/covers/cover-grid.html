The grid
Four vertical positions and one live-width box. Everything under them is free to change from book to book.
Oracle Veil · Book 1
Two-word title, so the second word takes signal and the strap carries the series name. Two reds, the cover maximum.
Darkwell · Book 2
One-word title, so the title stays bone and the strap carries the only red. The grid did not move.
Positions
Strap baseline 4% of trim height
Title band 38% to 73%
Author baseline 93%
Side margins 8% of trim width
Cap height follows width
Every title line is set to fill the 84% live width and the cap height falls out of that. This is why VEIL is taller than ORACLE: four letters filling the same measure as six. Setting both lines to one cap height and letting them end short is the fastest way to make a series look like it was assembled by different people.

The art in these three panels is drawn from the palette to prove the type grid holds for a two-word and a one-word title. It is not the Darkwell cover and not a proposal for one.
