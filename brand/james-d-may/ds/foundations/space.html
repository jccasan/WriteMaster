Geometry
The system is square. 2px is the maximum radius on any surface and the reticle is the only circle. Default control height is 48px and nothing drops below the 44px tap floor.
Rules
Hairlines are bone at 10% for quiet separation and 22% where an edge has to hold. Slate on ink measures 1.31:1, so a panel always needs one of the two.
