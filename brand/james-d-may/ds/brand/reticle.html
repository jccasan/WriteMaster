Rules
The reticle is the brand's only symbol. Do not add a second. It is never animated to track, sweep or target anything. It can fade and it can scale. It does not hunt. Below 32px the favicon is the glyph alone, because three letters in a ring at 16px is a grey smudge.

The monogram carries the one authorised variant dot: JDM occupies the centre, so the red dot moves below the letters at period weight. Every other mark keeps it at true centre.
