James D. May Books · Design System
06 — Cover grid

Covers

One locked grid for the series, measured off Book 1. Every number is a percentage of trim height, so it holds at 6×9 paperback and at 1600×2560 ebook without redrawing.

The grid

Four horizontal positions and one live-width box. Nothing else is fixed. The art underneath is free to change completely from book to book, which is the point of a grid.

Oracle Veil · Book 1

Two-word title, so the second word takes signal. Strap carries the series name in signal. Two red elements, which is the cover maximum.

Darkwell · Book 2

One-word title, so the title stays entirely bone and the strap carries the only red. One red element. The grid did not move.

The art in these three panels is a stand-in drawn from the palette, not the Darkwell cover and not a proposal for it. What it proves is that the type grid holds with a two-word title and a one-word title, and that a single-word title does not leave a hole.

Cap height follows width, not the other way round

Each title line is set to the full live width and the cap height falls out of that. This is why VEIL is taller than ORACLE on Book 1: four letters filling the same measure as six. Setting both lines to one cap height and letting them end short is the fastest way to make a series look like it was assembled by different people.

Series rules

The red word

The series name in the strap carries signal on every cover. A title of two or more words gives signal to one word, the second. A one-word title takes no title red at all. That yields two red elements on Oracle Veil and one on Darkwell, and nothing in the series ever reaches three.

What is locked and what is free

Locked: the four vertical positions, the 84% live width, the display face, the red-word rule, the author name at the foot.

Free: the art, the palette balance inside the six tokens, the number of title lines, where the light falls.

Spine and back

Spine: title in display 600 reading top to bottom, cap height 60% of spine width with a 24pt floor, author name at the head, JDM monogram at the foot 8mm from trim at 12mm.

Back cover: flat ink, no art. Blurb in the reading face at 11pt. Stacked wordmark at the foot, 32mm wide, one cap height clear of trim. Barcode sits in white, never in a tinted box.

Gap · the second series

Alex Guerrero (Slingshot, Shield) has no cover and no art direction. This grid will carry it, and the strap wording changes from ORACLE VEIL to ALEX GUERRERO. Whether the two series should be visually distinct on a shelf is an unanswered question and a commercial one. Do not solve it by inventing a second accent colour.
