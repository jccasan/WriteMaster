James D. May Books · Design System
03 — Typography

Type

Three faces. The display face never sets body copy. The reading face never appears in interface.

Display

2 weights
ORACLE
FACE  Druk Condensed
LIC   Commercial Type, desktop + web

Cover titles, the wordmark, site H1, spine, social headlines. All caps, tracking from -0.03em at display size to +0.22em when it goes small in a strap. Medium 600 for the wordmark and author name. Bold 700 for cover titles.

Interface

400 / 600 only
Preorder now
FACE  Inter
LIC   SIL OFL, free

Navigation, buttons, captions, metadata, form labels, TikTok captions. Two weights and no more. If a third weight feels necessary the hierarchy is wrong, not the type.

Reading

400 + italic
She counted the exits.
FACE  Source Serif 4
LIC   SIL OFL, free

Excerpts, long-form web copy, the blurb, interior text reference. One weight and its italic. It has a true italic rather than a slant, which matters for the thought-italics the books use constantly.

Rules

Always

Display is all caps. There are no lowercase forms in this system.

Tracking moves with size. Tight when large, open when small. A strap at 11px needs +0.28em or it turns into a smudge.

Reading copy holds 26px line height at 16px, which is a 1.63 ratio. Do not tighten it to fit.

Never

Display face in body copy. It is a poster face and it does not read at paragraph length.

Reading face anywhere in interface. No serif buttons, no serif nav, no serif labels.

A fourth family. Not for code, not for numerals, not for a single clever pull quote.

Gap · the display face is identified, not confirmed

Druk Condensed is the closest commercially licensable match to the lettering on the Book 1 cover, read off the rendered image. Nobody has checked it against the real font file. Ask whoever built the cover for their font list. That is a one-email answer and it beats any guess made from a JPEG. Until it comes back, Oswald SemiBold and Bold are the production substitutes and the web faces regardless, since Oswald is free and Druk web licensing is per-domain and priced accordingly.

Inter is specified because the brief names it. It works. A grotesk with a narrower cap, IBM Plex Sans or Söhne, would sit closer to the condensed display face. That is a preference, not a defect, and changing it is a decision for the author and not for this document.
