The signal ramp

Bone on the cover red measures 4.35:1. That fails WCAG AA for normal text, so a primary button filled with it is not accessible. The fix is two darker shades of the same hue. Same red, three values, one accent.

signal
#D4141B
Display and marks. The cover red, untouched.
signalFill
#C31219
Interactive fills. Bone on it reads 4.97:1.
signalPress
#A51015

The signal rule, in words

Signal appears once per screen. One word, or one button, or one reticle dot. Never two. The single exception is a book cover, which carries it twice and only twice: the title word and the series name in the strap. If you are looking at a composition and counting to three, something has to come out.

Gap · red text on dark

Signal on ink measures 3.05:1. The cover carries it at display size where that is fine. The series strap on the website would be roughly 11px, and there it fails. This system does not add a lighter red to solve that, because the brief forbids a second accent and a light tint reads as pink against ink. If red strap text is wanted on the site, someone has to approve a fourth signal value. Until then the web strap is bone.

Gap · the print numbers

The CMYK builds are press-ready starting points for coated stock, not colorimetric conversions. Pantone matches are visual nearest, made on screen. Neither has been checked against a physical chip or a proof. Do both before the first print run. Ink in particular needs a rich black build, and the value here assumes coated. On uncoated it will go flat.
