Retailer strip

Retailer logos are not yours to restyle

Pull the official assets from each retailer’s brand portal and use them at the size and clear space they specify. Do not recolour them to bone, do not knock them back to 40%, do not put them on a tinted plate. Where a logo would break the palette badly, use the name set in the UI face, as above. That is permitted and it is what the strip shows.
