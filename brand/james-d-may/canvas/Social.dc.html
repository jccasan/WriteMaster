Safe zones

The tinted bands on the last card are roughly where TikTok and Reels put their own interface: about 130px at the top, 480px at the foot and 120px down the right at 1080×1920, with a narrower reserve down the left. Treat those as approximations. The two apps differ from each other and both move the numbers between releases, so check the current safe-area spec before a campaign instead of trusting this page. Nothing that has to be read goes in a band, which is why the title card sets its type low and left rather than centred.

Captions

He had been doing this job for 4,000 years and he still filed the paperwork.

UI face, semibold, bone on a solid ink strip. Two lines maximum. No drop shadow, no outline, no per-word highlight animation. If the line does not fit in two, cut the line.

The cover on social

Never

Cropped through the title.

Tilted, at any angle, for any reason.

Mocked up as a 3D book with a spine and a page edge.

Behind a drop shadow, a glow, or a reflection on a glossy floor.

Always

Flat, square to frame, full trim visible including the strap.

On ink, or on art that came from the same shoot as the cover.

A 1px bone hairline at 22% if the cover edge would otherwise dissolve into the background.

The spoiler badge

Solid signal plate, bone UI caps, square. It spends the card’s one red, so a card carrying it sets its title entirely in bone. The reticle’s centre dot is part of the mark and does not count against the budget.
