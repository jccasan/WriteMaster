Corrections made in the master

Redrawn as vectorDone. Four SVG masters in brand/james-d-may/marks/. The reticle is final geometry. The type is live so it can be reviewed, and it gets converted to outlines against the licensed font file before release.
BOOKS letterspaced to the full width of the nameDone. BOOKS is locked to 572 units, the same optical width as JAMES D. MAY, so the B sits under the J and the S under the Y.
Tracking equalised between the letters of BOOKSDone. The spacing is distributed evenly across the word rather than applied per pair, which is what closed the tight K to S gap in the source.
Rule break gaps equalisedDone. Both gaps are 14 grid units, measured from the tick tip to the end of the rule. They are equal by construction now, not by eye.
Reticle ticksDecided: all four equal, 19 grid units each. A longer vertical pair looks better on a poster and falls apart at 16px, where this mark has to work as a favicon. Equal ticks hold their proportion all the way down. The horizontal pair stays readable because the rule stops 14 units short of each tip, so the break does the separating rather than the length.
Period after D at letter weightDone. It inherits from the face at a single weight, so it cannot drift when the wordmark is rescaled.

The reticle does not move

It is never animated to track, sweep, lock on, or target anything. It can fade and it can scale. It does not hunt.

Correction · the monogram is not the favicon

The brief puts JDM in the ring for favicon, avatar and spine. Three letters inside a ring at 16px is a grey smudge. The floor for the lettered monogram is 32px. Below that the favicon is the reticle alone with the red dot, which is the one element that still reads at 16px. Both are drawn above at true size so the difference is visible rather than argued.
