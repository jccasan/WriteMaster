Backgrounds are ink. Type is bone. Red appears once. Fog is light only, never above 24% opacity. Ember belongs to photographs. Buttons fill with #C31219, not #D4141B, because bone on the brighter red fails accessibility.

Every title line stretches to the full live width, so lines with fewer letters come out taller. That is correct.

Two-word titles put red on the second word. One-word titles stay bone and let the strap carry the red.

Never
Two red things on one screen. Covers take two. The dot inside the mark never counts.
A second accent colour, a second symbol, or a fourth typeface.
The display face in body copy, or the reading face in a button.
Rounded corners above 2px, gradients behind interface, shadows on the wordmark.
The cover tilted, cropped through the title, or mocked up as a 3D book.
Chrome, lens flare, glitch effects, gold, or a CLASSIFIED stamp.
Em dashes, exclamation points, or the word unputdownable.
If you are stuck

Choose restraint. Less type, more ink, one red. Nothing in this brand has been improved by adding something.

Check it in grey. If the layout stops working when the red goes grey, the red was holding up a hierarchy that size and space should have built.
